## New findings

### R2-BUG-1 (Low, UX, pre-existing): on a phone, the 412 stale notice appears off-screen above the form
- **Repro:** 390×844, Evening page, fill the form so that you scroll down to "Seal today's ledger". Hand-edit the day file, then tap Seal.
- **Expected:** the user sees that the save did not happen. Either the panel scrolls into view or receives focus, or a notice appears near the Seal button.
- **Actual:**
  - The panel renders at the top of the page, `getBoundingClientRect().top = -504` with `scrollY = 774`.
  - The viewport around the Seal button looks identical to before, and focus moves to `<body>` (`img/M2r2-02-evening-412-updated-390.png`, `img/M2r2-03-evening-412-conflict-390.png`).
  - Screen-reader users do get it, because the panel is in an `aria-live="polite"` region. Sighted users may assume the save succeeded.
  - The form still holds their text, so no data is lost unless they leave the page.
  - Retest 1 used full-page screenshots, which is why this wasn't seen earlier.
- **Suspected file:** `web/src/routes/Evening.svelte` (412 handler). Suggested fix: after setting the stale state, `scrollIntoView({block:'start'})` and focus the panel (`tabindex="-1"`), using `behavior: 'auto'` under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

- In Playwright, the energy lanterns' decorative `.cord` span intercepts clicks on the sr-only input. Clicking the label works, and a real tap lands on the label, so users aren't affected.
