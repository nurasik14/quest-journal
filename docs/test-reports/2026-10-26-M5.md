## Bugs (ranked)

### MED-1: The Market announces 3 sentences per purchase and 2 per undo (AC17 / designer-AC4)
- **Repro:** Market → tap "A café meal" → "Buy for 150 taels". Inspect `[aria-live],[role=status]`.
- **Expected:** exactly one live sentence per coin event (designer-AC4: "the a11y tree or live-region text after done, buy, and undo shows one sentence each").
- **Actual:**
  - Buy: `"Spent 150 silver taels. Balance 305."` (BalanceBadge/CoinBadge sr-only, `aria-live=polite`) + `"Prescribed. Take as directed."` (sticker caption `<p class="qj-sr-only" aria-live=polite>`) + `"Bought: A café meal. Undo"` (toast, `role=status`).
  - Undo: `"Returned 150 silver taels. Balance 455."` + `"Put back on the shelf. 150 taels returned."`.
  - The stale "Prescribed…" stays in its region.
  - Today is correct: one sentence.
- **Suspected:** `web/src/routes/Market.svelte` (the hero `BalanceBadge`/`CoinBadge` should get `announce={null}` the way Today does, or the toast should be the only voice) and the sticker-caption live `<p>`.

### LOW-1: After a 409 in the buy sheet, focus falls to `<body>` and the reason isn't announced
- **Repro:** Open the sheet for an item you can afford. Lower the balance on disk (hand-edit an adjustment). Tap "Buy for 300 taels".
- **Expected:** focus stays inside the dialog (on the Buy button or the note), and the note "You have 55 of 300 taels." is perceivable to a screen reader.
- **Actual:** `document.activeElement` is `<body>`. The disabled "Wrapping it up…" button drops focus, so the trap is broken until the user clicks. The note `<p class="note">` has no live role and no `aria-describedby`.
- **Suspected:** `Market.svelte` buy handler (restore focus to the Buy button after the request settles; put the note in a `role=status` or link it via `aria-describedby`).

### LOW-2: The `shop_buy` sticker is drawn above its row and covers the previous row's price chip
- **Repro:** at 390×844, buy "A treat" (the row below "Guilt-free gaming…").
- **Expected (screens §1.7 / AC20):** the sticker sits in the row gutter and never covers text or buttons.
- **Actual:**
  - The sticker box is `[234,597,133×134]` while the treat row starts at y=720.
  - For 4 s it covers the "60" price chip of the gaming row (`M5-15-market-bought-missing-sticker-390.png`).
- **Suspected:** `Market.svelte` `.sticker { bottom: calc(100% - var(--sp-3)) }`. It should anchor to the row's own box, e.g. `top`/`right` inside the row.

### LOW-3: The sheet shows a negative "Balance after" for items you can't buy
- **Repro:** with balance 55, open "An ML/technical book" (900).
- **Expected:** an explanation instead of an outcome that can't happen.
- **Actual:**
  - The sheet reads "Balance after: −845", then "You have 55 of 900 taels.", then an active red "Buy for 900 taels".
  - The same happens after a 409 ("Balance after: −245", `M5-08`).
  - The negative number reads like a debt preview, which the brief avoids.
- **Suspected:** `Market.svelte:358`. Show `balanceAfter` only when `balance >= price`.

### NITs
- **NIT-1:** Sub-lines skip the thousands separator: "needs 1045 more taels" next to a "1,500" price chip. Use `fmtTaels` in `Market.svelte`.
- **NIT-2:** The optimistic settled note briefly renders "Done. +0 taels. Sealed in the ledger." (~20 ms, seen in a MutationObserver log) before the server's `earned` arrives. Not visible in practice. `Today.svelte`/`QuestCard` could omit the amount until it's known.
- **NIT-3:** A let-go **boss** still shows a "100 silver taels" chip although it earns 0. AC18 only covers dailies, so this is a consistency nit.
- **NIT-4:** `CoinBadge`'s delta float honours `prefers-reduced-motion` but not `:root[data-motion="reduce"]`. `Market.svelte`'s sheet honours both.
- **NIT-5 (observation, code read, not reproduced):** `shop.purchase` gathers earnings **before** taking the `purchases.yaml` lock (commented as intentional). A quest undo racing a purchase could let the purchase pass on the pre-undo balance, so the balance ends below zero by at most that quest's tariff. The balance stays truthful; it can't be inflated.
