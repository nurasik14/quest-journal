### AI content in the card
- **designer-AC6:** AI quests render with the same `QuestCard` as pool quests. `why` (≤200 chars, Maomao's voice) shows in full with no clamp or ellipsis.

### Fallback & failure
- **designer-AC9:** Claude fails twice (stub exit 1, or invalid JSON twice) → the job **succeeds** with `source: "pool"` → the quests render, plus **one**
  `.qj-banner--fallback` above them using `vocab.FALLBACK_NOTE`, with no retry button. The banner shows only when generation *attempted* AI and fell back
  (for example `generation.fallback_reason` is set), not for pool-by-design days (M2 behaviour preserved). 🌙
- **designer-AC10:** If the job itself fails (or `needs_generation` after a server restart), you get the `ScrollCard variant="alert"` "The herbs didn't grind this time." + primary

  `error|failed|invalid` appear in rendered text.
- **designer-AC11:** A stub that hangs forever → the server timeout fires → the user sees the fallback (AC9) or "Try again" (AC10) within **timeout + 4 s**.

## 2. Risks: what the builder will likely get wrong
1. **Await-inline habit.** In M2, `jobs.run` was awaited inside the POST. If that stays, the phone hangs for 60 s on a spinner-less button, and Tailscale/iOS
   may drop the request. The 202 must come back before `claude -p` finishes.
2. **Loader announced on every poll.** Re-rendering `HerbLoader` every 2 s resets its phase timer (15 s/45 s never reached) and spams screen readers. Key the
   loader on the job id. The elapsed time is measured from `job.started_at`, not from mount, so navigating back doesn't restart at "grinding".
3. **Fallback banner on every pool day.** The builder may show `FALLBACK_NOTE` whenever `source === "pool"`. It must show only when AI was tried and fell back.
4. **Boss swap under the thumb.** Lazy pool boss + async AI boss means the card the user is reading changes. AC15 hides the placeholder while the job runs.
5. **Long Maomao prose.** AI `why` text is longer and less predictable than the seed pool. Unbroken strings or 3-line titles will collide with the 済 seal and Swap.
6. **Stale `canReroll`.** After a reroll, the other cards must lose Swap immediately (from the response's `rerolls_left`), not after a refetch.
7. **Leaking internals into copy.** `claude` stderr, zod messages and "timeout" are tempting to surface. None of them may reach rendered text (AC10).
8. **Loading copy drift.** M2 shipped the psychologist's "Maomao is choosing today's herbs…" in `COPY.generating`. The user's brief and `vocab.LOADING.generate`
   say **"Maomao is grinding herbs…"**. PROVISIONAL: use `vocab.LOADING.*` for all AI loaders so there's one source.
9. **Theme ownership.** Several items (C1 unroll, C5 seal space, S3 note slot, C2 seal lift, C4 caption) live in my theme files. See §3 for the
   PROVISIONAL rule.
